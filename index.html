The Zambian Narratives Project

The Zambian Narratives Project creates bilingual and multilingual children's books that help children and young people in Zambia and across the diaspora connect with language, culture, stories, and heritage.

Through culturally relevant storytelling, the project celebrates Zambia's rich linguistic and cultural diversity while supporting literacy, language learning, and cultural connection. The project includes a growing collection of children's books available in English and Zambia's national languages, helping young readers see themselves, their communities, and their cultures reflected in the stories they read.
