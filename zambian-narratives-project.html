The Zambian Narratives Project

Shining a light on why children's stories written in Zambia's national languages matter.

A Family Adventure

A bilingual children's book available in English and Zambia's seven national languages. It was written so that children can see their language, culture and families in the stories they read.

The project began from a passion for children's empowerment and a belief that representation starts on the page.
